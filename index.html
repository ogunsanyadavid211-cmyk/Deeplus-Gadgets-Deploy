Questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to know the exact gadget I want?

No. Tell us what you want to use it for and your budget. We can help you narrow the options.

Is submitting a visit request an automatic appointment?

No. The form sends your request to Deeplus. We review it and confirm the appointment details with you on WhatsApp.

Can I request delivery even if I do not know the exact model?

Yes. Tell us the type of gadget, what you need it for and your budget or preferred model. We can discuss suitable options.

Do you always have every product in stock?

No. Availability changes. We will tell you what is available and what needs to be sourced rather than pretending otherwise.

How does the free spin gift work?

After an eligible purchase is confirmed, you receive one spin for a free gift. Gifts are subject to availability and the rules of the purchase/gift system.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Complete the home delivery request above. We will confirm the gadget, price, delivery details and next steps with you.
